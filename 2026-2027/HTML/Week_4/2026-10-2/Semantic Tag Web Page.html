The why Spider-Man is my favorite Super Heroes

Spider-Man is one of the best superhero of the history and the favorite of a lot of people, not only my. I love Spider-Man because he is not just a superhero; he is also Peter Parker it means that he has two lives and two responsibilities. As Spider-Man, he has the responsibility of defending New York City, fighting powerful enemies, and saving the lives of people in danger each day. But as Peter Parker, he has to take care of his family, and also keep up with his job and university at the same time. So I admire him for all that and also because, as a superhero, he is amazing and unique; who would think that a man with spider abilities could be that amazing?
